Re: SN-2026-0104 — Worst-of Autocallable Contingent Coupon Notes with Memory linked to S&P 500 Index and EURO STOXX 50 Index
Further to our conversation, we are pleased to confirm the indicative terms of the above transaction. Northbridge Capital Markets (Canada) Inc. will issue Notes in a total Notional Amount of USD 2.5 million (USD 2,500,000) under its Structured Notes Programme, Series 2026. The Notes were traded on 09-Jun-2026, will be issued on 16-Jun-2026 and mature on 16-Jun-2028. The Notes are linked to the worst performing of the S&P 500 Index (Bloomberg: SPX Index) and the EURO STOXX 50 Index (Bloomberg: SX5E Index).
The Notes pay a conditional Coupon of 10.2% per annum, payable quarterly, payable quarterly in arrears, on each Coupon Payment Date on which the Coupon Condition is satisfied. Coupon amounts accrue on a 30/360 day count basis. Memory feature: if a Coupon is not paid on a Coupon Payment Date because the Coupon Condition is not satisfied, that Coupon is not lost. It will be paid, together with the Coupon then due, on the first subsequent Coupon Payment Date on which the Coupon Condition is satisfied.
Automatic Early Redemption: if on any Autocall Observation Date the closing level of the Underlying is at or above 100% of the Initial Level, the Notes redeem early at 100% of the Notional Amount plus the Coupon then due. Autocall Observation Dates: 09-Jun-2027, 09-Dec-2027, 09-Jun-2028.
Knock-in Event: the Notes are subject to a knock-in barrier observed on the Final Valuation Date only (European observation) at a Knock-in Level of 60% of the Initial Level. If a Knock-in Event has occurred and the Notes have not been redeemed early, the Final Redemption Amount will be reduced in line with the performance of the Underlying.
All payments are made in USD by cash settlement and payment dates follow the Following convention (Toronto, New York and London business days).
