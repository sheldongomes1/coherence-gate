INDICATIVE TERM SHEET — OTC INDEX OPTION
European Cash-Settled Call Option on the Bloomberg Versa 10 Index  ·  Reference OP-2026-0121  ·  Dated 20 July 2026
1. Summary of the Transaction
Northbridge Capital Markets (Canada) Inc. ("Party A", the Seller) will sell to Lakeshore Life Insurance Company ("Party B", the Buyer) a European-style, cash-settled call option (the "Transaction") on the Bloomberg Versa 10 Index (Bloomberg: BVERSA10 Index) (the "Index"). The Buyer has issued fixed indexed annuity products under which retail policyholders are credited with participation in the upside performance of the Index; the Buyer accordingly bears exposure to appreciation of the Index and enters into the Transaction to hedge those index-linked crediting obligations, with the option terms (observation, participation and term) designed to match the crediting parameters of the relevant annuity segment. This term sheet summarises indicative terms; the Transaction will be governed by, and the definitive terms set out in, a Confirmation under the ISDA Master Agreement between the parties, incorporating the 2002 ISDA Equity Derivatives Definitions.
2. The Underlying Index
The Index is a rules-based, volatility-targeting index administered by Bloomberg Index Services Limited ("BISL") and calculated in accordance with the Bloomberg Versa Indices Methodology dated 21 March 2025 (the "Index Methodology"), as supplemented by the index-specific document for the Index. The parameters below summarise the index-specific terms relevant to the Transaction; in the event of any inconsistency, the Index Methodology and the index-specific document prevail.

4. Settlement Amount
If the Transaction is exercised (including by Automatic Exercise), the Seller shall pay to the Buyer on the Cash Settlement Payment Date an amount determined by the Calculation Agent as follows:
Settlement Amount = Notional Amount × Participation Rate × Max (0, (Final Index Level − Strike Level) / Initial Index Level)
where the Final Index Level is the official closing level of the Index on the Valuation Date, as published by the Index Administrator. If the Final Index Level is at or below the Strike Level, no amount is payable and the Transaction expires worthless.

Risk Factors (Summary)
This Transaction involves significant risks and is suitable only for sophisticated counterparties. The Buyer's maximum loss is the Premium paid. The Index employs a volatility-targeting mechanism which may materially reduce exposure to its underlying index in periods of elevated volatility, including in rising markets; the Deduction Factor and Transaction Cost Rate reduce the Index Value on an ongoing basis; and as an Excess Return index the Index does not benefit from any cash or reinvestment return. Each of these features may materially reduce the probability and magnitude of a positive Settlement Amount relative to an option on an unadjusted market index. Past performance, including back-tested performance, of the Index is not a reliable indicator of future performance. The parties are exposed to each other's credit risk to the extent not collateralised.
