TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF THE NOTES
Northbridge Capital Markets (Canada) Inc. — Structured Notes Programme, Series 2026
Tranche reference SN-2026-0103

1. Issuer. The Notes are issued by Northbridge Capital Markets (Canada) Inc..

2. Notional Amount and Currency. The aggregate Notional Amount is 8,000,000 CAD. The Specified Currency is CAD and all payments under the Notes are made in CAD.

3. Dates. Trade Date: March 3, 2026. Issue Date: March 10, 2026. Maturity Date: March 12, 2029, subject to adjustment in accordance with Condition 9.

4. Underlying. The Notes are linked to the worst performing of the S&P/TSX 60 Index (Bloomberg: SPTSX60 Index) and the BVERSA10 Index (Bloomberg: BVERSA10 Index). The Initial Level is 100 per cent. of the official closing level of each Underlying on the Trade Date.

5. Coupon. Subject to the Coupon Condition, the Notes bear a Coupon of 6.8 per cent. per annum, payable quarterly. Coupons are payable quarterly in arrear and are calculated on the basis of Actual/360.

6. Memory. Memory feature: if a Coupon is not paid on a Coupon Payment Date because the Coupon Condition is not satisfied, that Coupon is not lost. It will be paid, together with the Coupon then due, on the first subsequent Coupon Payment Date on which the Coupon Condition is satisfied.

7. Automatic Early Redemption. Automatic Early Redemption: if on any Autocall Observation Date the closing level of the Underlying is at or above 100 per cent. of the Initial Level, the Notes redeem early at 100% of the Notional Amount plus the Coupon then due. Autocall Observation Dates: March 3, 2027, September 3, 2027, March 3, 2028, September 5, 2028.

8. Knock-in. Knock-in Event: the Notes are subject to a knock-in barrier observed on the Final Valuation Date only (European observation) at a Knock-in Level of 70 per cent. of the Initial Level. If a Knock-in Event has occurred and the Notes have not been redeemed early, the Final Redemption Amount will be reduced in line with the performance of the Underlying.

9. Business Day Convention. Where any payment date would otherwise fall on a day that is not a Business Day, it shall be adjusted in accordance with the Modified Following Business Day Convention. Business Days are days on which commercial banks are open in Toronto, New York and London.

10. Settlement. Cash settlement.

11. Fees. Fees and distribution: the Issue Price includes distribution fees of up to 1.50% of the Notional Amount payable to the distributor. The Issuer's hedging costs are estimated at 0.35% per annum. These amounts are not Coupons and are not payable to Noteholders.

12. Risk. RISK FACTORS (SUMMARY). An investment in the Notes involves risks. Investors may lose some or all of their investment. The Notes are unsecured obligations of the Issuer and are subject to the credit risk of the Issuer. The secondary market, if any, may be illiquid; a bid price, where available, may be up to 3.00% below the theoretical value of the Notes. Past performance of the Underlying is not a reliable indicator of future performance. A 10% decline in the Underlying does not translate into a 10% decline in the value of the Notes; the relationship is non-linear and depends on remaining maturity, volatility and interest rates.

13. General. This document is an indicative term sheet and does not constitute an offer. The Notes will be issued under the Issuer's Structured Notes Programme, Series 2026 and are subject to the Base Prospectus and the applicable Final Terms. In the event of inconsistency between this term sheet and the Final Terms, the Final Terms prevail. Not for distribution in the United States or to U.S. persons. Governing law: Ontario. Calculation Agent: the Issuer.
