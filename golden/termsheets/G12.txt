Re: SN-2026-0112 — Autocallable Note on BVERSA10 Index

Further to our conversation, we are pleased to confirm the indicative terms of the above transaction. Northbridge Capital Markets (Canada) Inc. will issue Notes in a total Notional Amount of CAD 6 million (CAD 6,000,000) under its Structured Notes Programme, Series 2026. The Notes were traded on 24-Mar-2026, will be issued on 31-Mar-2026 and mature on 02-Apr-2029.

The Notes pay a conditional coupon of 6.1% per annum, payable semi-annually, accruing on a Actual/365 (Fixed) basis. No memory feature: a Coupon that is not paid because the Coupon Condition is not satisfied on the relevant Coupon Observation Date is forfeited and will not be paid at a later date.

Early redemption: the Notes are not subject to automatic early redemption.

Knock-in Event: the Notes are subject to a knock-in barrier observed on the Final Valuation Date only (European observation) at a Knock-in Level of 65% of the Initial Level. If a Knock-in Event has occurred and the Notes have not been redeemed early, the Final Redemption Amount will be reduced in line with the performance of the Underlying.

All payments are made in CAD by cash settlement and payment dates follow the Following convention (Toronto, New York and London business days).
