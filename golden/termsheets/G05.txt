INDICATIVE TERM SHEET

Northbridge Capital Markets (Canada) Inc.
Structured Notes Programme, Series 2026

Autocallable Barrier Note linked to S&P 500 Index
Reference: SN-2026-0105
Dated 17 February 2026

1. Summary of the transaction

Northbridge Capital Markets (Canada) Inc. (the "Issuer") will issue notes (the "Notes") with reference SN-2026-0105 in an aggregate Notional Amount of USD 10,000,000. The Notes are linked to the performance of the S&P 500 Index (Bloomberg: SPX Index). The Trade Date is 17 February 2026, the Issue Date is 24 February 2026 and the Scheduled Maturity Date is 24 February 2028, subject to the Business Day Convention below.

The Initial Level of each Underlying is 100% of its official closing level on the Trade Date.

2. Coupon

The Notes pay a conditional Coupon of 5.25% per annum, payable annually, on each Coupon Payment Date on which the Coupon Condition is satisfied. Coupon amounts accrue on a 30/360 day count basis. No memory feature: a Coupon that is not paid because the Coupon Condition is not satisfied on the relevant Coupon Observation Date is forfeited and will not be paid at a later date.

3. Early redemption and barrier

Early redemption: the Notes are not subject to automatic early redemption.

Capital protection: the Notes are not subject to a knock-in barrier. Redemption at maturity is at 100% of the Notional Amount irrespective of the Final Level.

4. Settlement

Settlement at maturity or upon early redemption is in cash, in USD. Payment dates are adjusted in accordance with the Modified Following Business Day Convention. Business Days: Toronto, New York and London.

5. Other

Fees and distribution: the Issue Price includes distribution fees of up to 1.50% of the Notional Amount payable to the distributor. The Issuer's hedging costs are estimated at 0.35% per annum. These amounts are not Coupons and are not payable to Noteholders.

RISK FACTORS (SUMMARY). An investment in the Notes involves risks. Investors may lose some or all of their investment. The Notes are unsecured obligations of the Issuer and are subject to the credit risk of the Issuer. The secondary market, if any, may be illiquid; a bid price, where available, may be up to 3.00% below the theoretical value of the Notes. Past performance of the Underlying is not a reliable indicator of future performance. A 10% decline in the Underlying does not translate into a 10% decline in the value of the Notes; the relationship is non-linear and depends on remaining maturity, volatility and interest rates.

This document is an indicative term sheet and does not constitute an offer. In the event of inconsistency between this term sheet and the Final Terms, the Final Terms prevail. Not for distribution in the United States or to U.S. persons. Governing law: Ontario.
