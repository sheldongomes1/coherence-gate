2. The Underlying Index
The Underlying is the EURO STOXX 50 Index (Bloomberg: SX5E Index) The Initial Level of each Underlying is 100.00% of its official closing level on the Trade Date, as published by the relevant index sponsor. Index Adjustment Events, Index Disruption Events and successor-index provisions apply as set out in the Base Prospectus.
3. Notes to the Key Terms
Memory feature: if a Coupon is not paid on a Coupon Payment Date because the Coupon Condition is not satisfied, that Coupon is not lost. It will be paid, together with the Coupon then due, on the first subsequent Coupon Payment Date on which the Coupon Condition is satisfied.
Knock-in Event: the Notes are subject to a knock-in barrier observed continuously on each Scheduled Trading Day from the Trade Date to the Final Valuation Date (American observation) at a Knock-in Level of 60.00% of the Initial Level. If a Knock-in Event has occurred and the Notes have not been redeemed early, the Final Redemption Amount will be reduced in line with the performance of the Underlying.
Automatic Early Redemption: if on any Autocall Observation Date the closing level of the Underlying is at or above 100.00% of the Initial Level, the Notes redeem early at 100% of the Notional Amount plus the Coupon then due. Autocall Observation Dates: 2027-05-12, 2027-11-12, 2028-05-12.
4. Fees and Other Terms
The Issue Price of 100% includes distribution fees of up to 1.50% of the Notional Amount payable to the distributor. The Issuer's estimated hedging costs are 0.35% per annum. These amounts are not Coupons and are not payable to Noteholders. Calculation Agent: the Issuer. Governing law: Ontario. Minimum denomination: EUR 10,000.
Risk Factors (Summary)
An investment in the Notes involves significant risks. Investors may lose some or all of their investment. The Notes are unsecured obligations of the Issuer and are subject to the credit risk of the Issuer. Past performance, including back-tested performance, of the Underlying is not a reliable indicator of future performance. The secondary market, if any, may be illiquid; a bid price, where available, may be up to 3.00% below the theoretical value of the Notes. A 10% decline in the Underlying does not translate into a 10% decline in the value of the Notes; the relationship is non-linear. The memory feature does not guarantee that any deferred Coupon will ultimately be paid.
