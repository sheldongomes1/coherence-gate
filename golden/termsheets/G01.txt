INDICATIVE TERM SHEET
Autocallable Contingent Coupon Notes linked to S&P 500 Index  ·  Reference SN-2026-0101  ·  Dated April 14, 2026
1. The Transaction
Northbridge Capital Markets (Canada) Inc. (the "Issuer") will issue notes (the "Notes") with reference SN-2026-0101 in an aggregate Notional Amount of 5,000,000 USD, linked to the performance of the S&P 500 Index (Bloomberg: SPX Index). The Trade Date is April 14, 2026; the Issue Date is April 21, 2026; the Scheduled Maturity Date is April 23, 2029, subject to adjustment in accordance with the Modified Following Business Day Convention (Business Days: Toronto, New York and London). The Initial Level of each Underlying is 100 per cent. of its official closing level on the Trade Date.
2. Coupon
The Notes pay a conditional Coupon of 7.5 per cent. per annum, payable quarterly, payable quarterly in arrears, on each Coupon Payment Date on which the Coupon Condition is satisfied. Coupon amounts accrue on a 30/360 day count basis. No memory feature: a Coupon that is not paid because the Coupon Condition is not satisfied on the relevant Coupon Observation Date is forfeited and will not be paid at a later date.
3. Automatic Early Redemption
Automatic Early Redemption: if on any Autocall Observation Date the closing level of the Underlying is at or above 100 per cent. of the Initial Level, the Notes redeem early at 100% of the Notional Amount plus the Coupon then due. Autocall Observation Dates: April 14, 2027, October 14, 2027, April 14, 2028, October 16, 2028.
4. Knock-in
Knock-in Event: the Notes are subject to a knock-in barrier observed on the Final Valuation Date only (European observation) at a Knock-in Level of 65 per cent. of the Initial Level. If a Knock-in Event has occurred and the Notes have not been redeemed early, the Final Redemption Amount will be reduced in line with the performance of the Underlying.
5. Settlement
Cash settlement in USD.
6. The Underlying Index
The Underlying is the S&P 500 Index (Bloomberg: SPX Index) The Initial Level of each Underlying is 100 per cent. of its official closing level on the Trade Date, as published by the relevant index sponsor. Index Adjustment Events, Index Disruption Events and successor-index provisions apply as set out in the Base Prospectus.

8. Fees and Other Terms
The Issue Price of 100% includes distribution fees of up to 1.50% of the Notional Amount payable to the distributor. The Issuer's estimated hedging costs are 0.35% per annum. These amounts are not Coupons and are not payable to Noteholders. Calculation Agent: the Issuer. Governing law: Ontario. Minimum denomination: USD 10,000.
Risk Factors (Summary)
An investment in the Notes involves significant risks. Investors may lose some or all of their investment. The Notes are unsecured obligations of the Issuer and are subject to the credit risk of the Issuer. Past performance, including back-tested performance, of the Underlying is not a reliable indicator of future performance. The secondary market, if any, may be illiquid; a bid price, where available, may be up to 3.00% below the theoretical value of the Notes. A 10% decline in the Underlying does not translate into a 10% decline in the value of the Notes; the relationship is non-linear.
