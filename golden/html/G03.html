INDICATIVE TERM SHEET
Worst-of Autocallable Contingent Coupon Notes with Memory linked to S&P/TSX 60 Index and Bloomberg Versa 10 Index  ·  Reference SN-2026-0103  ·  Dated 3 March 2026

1. Summary of the Transaction

Northbridge Capital Markets (Canada) Inc. (the "Issuer") will issue notes (the "Notes") with reference SN-2026-0103 in an aggregate Notional Amount of CAD 8,000,000. The Notes are linked to the performance of the worst performing of the S&P/TSX 60 Index (Bloomberg: SPTSX60 Index) and the Bloomberg Versa 10 Index (Bloomberg: BVERSA10 Index). The Trade Date is 3 March 2026, the Issue Date is 10 March 2026 and the Scheduled Maturity Date is 12 March 2029, in each case subject to the Business Day Convention set out below. The Initial Level of the Underlying is 100% of its official closing level on the Trade Date, as published by the Index Administrator.

2. The Underlying Index

The Underlying is a rules-based, volatility-targeting index administered by Bloomberg Index Services Limited ("BISL") and calculated in accordance with the Bloomberg Versa Indices Methodology dated 21 March 2025 (the "Index Methodology"), as supplemented by the index-specific document for the Underlying. The parameters below summarise the index-specific terms relevant to the Notes; in the event of any inconsistency, the Index Methodology and the index-specific document prevail.

The Index Value is floored at zero; if the Index Value reaches zero it will remain at zero. Market disruptions affecting the Underlying are addressed in accordance with the Index Methodology and the BISL Benchmark Procedures Handbook.

3. Coupon

The Notes pay a conditional Coupon of 6.8% per annum, payable quarterly, payable quarterly in arrears, on each Coupon Payment Date on which the Coupon Condition is satisfied. Coupon amounts accrue on a Actual/360 day count basis. Memory feature: if a Coupon is not paid on a Coupon Payment Date because the Coupon Condition is not satisfied, that Coupon is not lost. It will be paid, together with the Coupon then due, on the first subsequent Coupon Payment Date on which the Coupon Condition is satisfied.

4. Automatic Early Redemption and Barrier

Automatic Early Redemption: if on any Autocall Observation Date the closing level of the Underlying is at or above 100% of the Initial Level, the Notes redeem early at 100% of the Notional Amount plus the Coupon then due. Autocall Observation Dates: 3 March 2027, 3 September 2027, 3 March 2028, 5 September 2028.

Knock-in Event: the Notes are subject to a knock-in barrier observed on the Final Valuation Date only (European observation) at a Knock-in Level of 70% of the Initial Level. If a Knock-in Event has occurred and the Notes have not been redeemed early, the Final Redemption Amount will be reduced in line with the performance of the Underlying.

5. Settlement

Settlement at maturity or upon automatic early redemption is in cash, in CAD. Payment dates are adjusted in accordance with the Modified Following Business Day Convention. Business Days: Toronto, New York and London.

6. Fees and Other Terms

The Issue Price of 100% includes distribution fees of up to 1.50% of the Notional Amount payable to the distributor. The Issuer's estimated hedging costs are 0.35% per annum. These amounts are not Coupons and are not payable to Noteholders. Calculation Agent: the Issuer. Governing law: Ontario. Minimum denomination: CAD 10,000.

Risk Factors (Summary)

An investment in the Notes involves significant risks. Investors may lose some or all of their investment. The Notes are unsecured obligations of the Issuer and are subject to the credit risk of the Issuer. The Underlying employs a volatility-targeting mechanism which may materially reduce exposure to its underlying index in periods of elevated volatility, including in rising markets, and the Deduction Factor and Transaction Cost Rate reduce the Index Value on an ongoing basis; as an Excess Return index, the Underlying does not benefit from any cash or reinvestment return. Past performance, including back-tested performance, of the Underlying is not a reliable indicator of future performance. The secondary market, if any, may be illiquid; a bid price, where available, may be up to 3.00% below the theoretical value of the Notes. A 10% decline in the Underlying does not translate into a 10% decline in the value of the Notes; the relationship is non-linear. The memory feature does not guarantee that any deferred Coupon will ultimately be paid.
