## 2. The Underlying Index

The Underlying is the S&P/TSX 60 Index (Bloomberg: SPTSX60 Index) The Initial Level of each Underlying is 100.00% of its official closing level on the Trade Date, as published by the relevant index sponsor. Index Adjustment Events, Index Disruption Events and successor-index provisions apply as set out in the Base Prospectus.

## 3. Notes to the Key Terms

No memory feature: a Coupon that is not paid because the Coupon Condition is not satisfied on the relevant Coupon Observation Date is forfeited and will not be paid at a later date.

Knock-in Event: the Notes are subject to a knock-in barrier observed on the Final Valuation Date only (European observation) at a Knock-in Level of 65.00% of the Initial Level. If a Knock-in Event has occurred and the Notes have

not been redeemed early, the Final Redemption Amount will be reduced in line with the performance of the Underlying.

Automatic Early Redemption: if on any Autocall Observation Date the closing level of the Underlying is at or above 100.00% of the Initial Level, the Notes redeem early at 100% of the Notional Amount plus the Coupon then due. Autocall Observation Dates: 2027-07-07, 2028-07-07.

## 4. Fees and Other Terms

The Issue Price of 100% includes distribution fees of up to 1.50% of the Notional Amount payable to the distributor. The Issuer's estimated hedging costs are 0.35% per annum. These amounts are not Coupons and are not payable to Noteholders. Calculation Agent: the Issuer. Governing law: Ontario. Minimum denomination: USD 10,000.
