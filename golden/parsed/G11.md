## INDICATIVE TERM SHEET

Worst-of Autocallable Contingent Coupon Notes with Memory linked to S&P 500 Index and S&P/TSX 60 Index · Reference SN-2026-0111 · Dated 26 May 2026

## 1. Summary of the Transaction

Northbridge Capital Markets (Canada) Inc. (the "Issuer") will issue notes (the "Notes") with reference SN-2026-0111 in an aggregate Notional Amount of USD 4,500,000. The Notes are linked to the performance of the worst performing of the S&P 500 Index (Bloomberg: SPX Index) and the S&P/TSX 60 Index (Bloomberg: SPTSX60 Index). The Trade Date is 26 May 2026, the Issue Date is 2 June 2026 and the Scheduled Maturity Date is 2 June 2028, in each case subject to the Business Day Convention set out below. The Initial Level of the Underlying is 100% of its official closing level on the Trade Date.

## 2. The Underlying Index

The Underlying is the worst performing of the S&P 500 Index (Bloomberg: SPX Index) and the S&P/TSX 60 Index (Bloomberg: SPTSX60 Index) The Initial Level of each Underlying is 100% of its official closing level on the Trade Date, as published by the relevant index sponsor. Index Adjustment Events, Index Disruption Events and successor- index provisions apply as set out in the Base Prospectus.

## 3. Coupon

The Notes pay a conditional Coupon of 8.4% per annum, payable quarterly, payable quarterly in arrears, on each Coupon Payment Date on which the Coupon Condition is satisfied. Coupon amounts accrue on a 30/360 day count basis. Memory feature: if a Coupon is not paid on a Coupon Payment Date because the Coupon Condition is not satisfied, that Coupon is not lost. It will be paid, together with the Coupon then due, on the first subsequent Coupon Payment Date on which the Coupon Condition is satisfied.

## 4. Automatic Early Redemption and Barrier

Automatic Early Redemption: if on any Autocall Observation Date the closing level of the Underlying is at or above 100% of the Initial Level, the Notes redeem early at 100% of the Notional Amount plus the Coupon then due. Autocall Observation Dates: 26 May 2027, 26 November 2027.

Knock-in Event: the Notes are subject to a knock-in barrier observed on the Final Valuation Date only (European observation) at a Knock-in Level of 60% of the Initial Level. If a Knock-in Event has occurred and the Notes have not been redeemed early, the Final Redemption Amount will be reduced in line with the performance of the Underlying.

## 5. Settlement

Settlement at maturity or upon automatic early redemption is in cash, in USD. Payment dates are adjusted in accordance with the Modified Following Business Day Convention. Business Days: Toronto, New York and London.

## 6. Fees and Other Terms

The Issue Price of 100% includes distribution fees of up to 1.50% of the Notional Amount payable to the distributor. The Issuer's estimated hedging costs are 0.35% per annum. These amounts are not Coupons and are not payable to Noteholders. Calculation Agent: the Issuer. Governing law: Ontario. Minimum denomination: USD 10,000.
