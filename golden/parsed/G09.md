Structured Notes Programme, Series 2026 · Global Markets — Equity & Index Solutions

## INDICATIVE TERM SHEET

Autocallable Contingent Coupon Notes linked to Bloomberg Versa 10 Index · Reference SN-2026-0109 · Dated April 28, 2026

## 1. The Transaction

Northbridge Capital Markets (Canada) Inc. (the "Issuer") will issue notes (the "Notes") with reference SN-2026-0109 in an aggregate Notional Amount of 5,000,000 CAD, linked to the performance of the Bloomberg Versa 10 Index (Bloomberg: BVERSA10 Index) (the "Underlying"). The Trade Date is April 28, 2026; the Issue Date is May 5, 2026; the Scheduled Maturity Date is May 7, 2029, subject to adjustment in accordance with the Modified Following Business Day Convention (Business Days: Toronto, New York and London). The Initial Level of each Underlying is 100 per cent. of its official closing level on the Trade Date.

## 2. Coupon

The Notes pay a conditional Coupon of 2.0625% per quarter (equivalent to 8.25% per annum), payable quarterly in arrears, on each Coupon Payment Date on which the Coupon Condition is satisfied. Coupon amounts accrue on a 30/360 day count basis. No memory feature: a Coupon that is not paid because the Coupon Condition is not satisfied on the relevant Coupon Observation Date is forfeited and will not be paid at a later date.

## 3. Automatic Early Redemption

Automatic Early Redemption: if on any Autocall Observation Date the closing level of the Underlying is at or above 100 per cent. of the Initial Level, the Notes redeem early at 100% of the Notional Amount plus the Coupon then due. Autocall Observation Dates: April 28, 2027, October 28, 2027, April 28, 2028, October 30, 2028.

## 4. Knock-in

Knock-in Event: the Notes are subject to a knock-in barrier observed on the Final Valuation Date only (European observation) at a Knock-in Level of 70 per cent. of the Initial Level. If a Knock-in Event has occurred and the Notes have not been redeemed early, the Final Redemption Amount will be reduced in line with the performance of the Underlying.

## 5. Settlement

Cash settlement in CAD.

## 8. Fees and Other Terms

The Issue Price of 100% includes distribution fees of up to 1.50% of the Notional Amount payable to the distributor. The Issuer's estimated hedging costs are 0.35% per annum. These amounts are not Coupons and are not payable to Noteholders. Calculation Agent: the Issuer. Governing law: Ontario. Minimum denomination: CAD 10,000.

## Risk Factors (Summary)

An investment in the Notes involves significant risks. Investors may lose some or all of their investment. The Notes are unsecured obligations of the Issuer and are subject to the credit risk of the Issuer. The Underlying employs a volatility-targeting mechanism which may materially reduce exposure to its underlying index in periods of elevated volatility, including in rising markets, and the Deduction Factor and Transaction Cost Rate reduce the Index Value on an ongoing basis; as an Excess Return index, the Underlying does not benefit from any cash or reinvestment return. Past performance, including back-tested performance, of the Underlying is not a reliable indicator of future performance. The secondary market, if any, may be illiquid; a bid price, where available, may be up to 3.00% below the theoretical value of the Notes. A 10% decline in the Underlying does not translate into a 10% decline in the value of the Notes; the relationship is non-linear.
