## NORTHBRIDGE CAPITAL MARKETS (CANADA) INC.

Structured Notes Programme, Series 2026 · Global Markets — Equity & Index Solutions

## INDICATIVE TERM SHEET

Autocallable Contingent Coupon Notes with Memory linked to EURO STOXX 50 Index · Reference SN-2026-0107 · Dated 20 January 2026

## 1. Summary of the Transaction

Northbridge Capital Markets (Canada) Inc. (the "Issuer") will issue notes (the "Notes") with reference SN-2026-0107 in an aggregate Notional Amount of EUR 6,000,000. The Notes are linked to the performance of the EURO STOXX 50 Index (Bloomberg: SX5E Index). The Trade Date is 20 January 2026, the Issue Date is 27 January 2026 and the Scheduled Maturity Date is 29 January 2029, in each case subject to the Business Day Convention set out below. The Initial Level of the Underlying is 100% of its official closing level on the Trade Date.

## 2. The Underlying Index

The Underlying is the EURO STOXX 50 Index (Bloomberg: SX5E Index) The Initial Level of each Underlying is 100% of its official closing level on the Trade Date, as published by the relevant index sponsor. Index Adjustment Events, Index Disruption Events and successor-index provisions apply as set out in the Base Prospectus.

## 3. Coupon

The Notes pay a conditional Coupon of 8% per annum, payable annually, payable annually in arrears, on each Coupon Payment Date on which the Coupon Condition is satisfied. Coupon amounts accrue on a 30/360 day count basis. Memory feature: if a Coupon is not paid on a Coupon Payment Date because the Coupon Condition is not satisfied, that Coupon is not lost. It will be paid, together with the Coupon then due, on the first subsequent Coupon Payment Date on which the Coupon Condition is satisfied.

## 4. Automatic Early Redemption and Barrier

Automatic Early Redemption: if on an Autocall Observation Date the closing level of the Underlying is at or above the applicable Autocall Level, the Notes redeem early at 100% of the Notional Amount plus the Coupon then due. Autocall Observation Dates and Autocall Levels (as a percentage of the Initial Level): 20 January 2027 at 100%, 20 January 2028 at 95%, 20 July 2028 at 90%.

Knock-in Event: the Notes are subject to a knock-in barrier observed on the Final Valuation Date only (European observation) at a Knock-in Level of 55% of the Initial Level. If a Knock-in Event has occurred and the Notes have not been redeemed early, the Final Redemption Amount will be reduced in line with the performance of the Underlying.

## 5. Settlement

Settlement at maturity or upon automatic early redemption is in cash, in EUR. Payment dates are adjusted in accordance with the Following Business Day Convention. Business Days: Toronto, New York and London.

## 6. Fees and Other Terms

The Issue Price of 100% includes distribution fees of up to 1.50% of the Notional Amount payable to the distributor. The Issuer's estimated hedging costs are 0.35% per annum. These amounts are not Coupons and are not payable to Noteholders. Calculation Agent: the Issuer. Governing law: Ontario. Minimum denomination: EUR 10,000.

## Risk Factors (Summary)

An investment in the Notes involves significant risks. Investors may lose some or all of their investment. The Notes are unsecured obligations of the Issuer and are subject to the credit risk of the Issuer. Past performance, including back-tested performance, of the Underlying is not a reliable indicator of future performance. The secondary market, if any, may be illiquid; a bid price, where available, may be up to 3.00% below the theoretical value of the Notes. A 10% decline in the Underlying does not translate into a 10% decline in the value of the Notes; the relationship is non-linear. The memory feature does not guarantee that any deferred Coupon will ultimately be paid.
