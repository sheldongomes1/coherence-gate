Autocallable Contingent Coupon Notes with Memory linked to Bloomberg Versa 10 Index · Reference SN-2026-0112 · Dated 8 May 2026

## 1. Summary of the Transaction

Northbridge Capital Markets (Canada) Inc. (the "Issuer") will issue notes (the "Notes") with reference SN-2026-0112 in an aggregate Notional Amount of USD 7,500,000. The Notes are linked to the performance of the Bloomberg Versa 10 Index (Bloomberg: BVERSA10 Index) (the "Underlying"). The Trade Date is 8 May 2026, the Issue Date is 15 May 2026 and the Scheduled Maturity Date is 17 May 2030, in each case subject to the Business Day Convention set out below. The Initial Level of the Underlying is 100% of its official closing level on the Trade Date, as published by the Index Administrator.

## 2. The Underlying Index

## 3. Coupon

The Notes pay a conditional Coupon of 8.25% per annum, payable quarterly, payable quarterly in arrears, provided the closing level of the Underlying on the relevant Coupon Observation Date is at or above the Coupon Barrier of 70% of the Initial Level. Coupon amounts accrue on a 30/360 day count basis. Memory feature: if a Coupon is not paid on a Coupon Payment Date because the Coupon Condition is not satisfied, that Coupon is not lost. It will be

## 4. Automatic Early Redemption and Barrier

Automatic Early Redemption: if on any Autocall Observation Date the closing level of the Underlying is at or above 100% of the Initial Level, the Notes redeem early at 100% of the Notional Amount plus the Coupon then due. Autocall Observation Dates: 10 May 2027, 8 November 2027, 8 May 2028, 8 November 2028, 8 May 2029, 8 November 2029.

Knock-in Event: the Notes are subject to a knock-in barrier observed on the Final Valuation Date only (European observation) at a Knock-in Level of 60% of the Initial Level. If a Knock-in Event has occurred and the Notes have not been redeemed early, the Final Redemption Amount will be reduced in line with the performance of the Underlying.

## 5. Settlement

Settlement at maturity or upon automatic early redemption is in cash, in USD. Payment dates are adjusted in accordance with the Modified Following Business Day Convention. Business Days: Toronto, New York and London. Final Valuation Date: 10 May 2030.

## 6. Fees and Other Terms

The Issue Price of 100% includes distribution fees of up to 1.25% of the Notional Amount payable to the distributor. The Issuer's estimated hedging costs are 0.30% per annum. These amounts are not Coupons and are not payable to Noteholders. Calculation Agent: the Issuer. Governing law: Ontario. Minimum denomination: USD 10,000.

## Risk Factors (Summary)

An investment in the Notes involves significant risks. Investors may lose some or all of their investment. The Notes are unsecured obligations of the Issuer and are subject to the credit risk of the Issuer. The Underlying employs a volatility-targeting mechanism which may materially reduce exposure to its underlying index in periods of elevated volatility, including in rising markets, and the Deduction Factor and Transaction Cost Rate reduce the Index Value on an ongoing basis; as an Excess Return index, the Underlying does not benefit from any cash or reinvestment return. Past performance, including back-tested performance, of the Underlying is not a reliable indicator of future performance. The secondary market, if any, may be illiquid; a bid price, where available, may be up to 3.00% below the theoretical value of the Notes. A 10% decline in the Underlying does not translate into a 10% decline in the value of the Notes; the relationship is non-linear. The memory feature does not guarantee that any deferred Coupon will ultimately be paid.
