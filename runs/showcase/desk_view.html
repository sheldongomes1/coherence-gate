Risk figures (delta, delta USD-equivalent, vega, FX) are indicative fixtures for the demo book and are not computed by this system. Convention: the delta of the booked position from the desk's side (the desk sold the call and issued the note, so both are short the index); the hedge is the mirror image and is not shown. The trust column asserts direction of concern, never magnitude. Trust states: ATTESTED — booking attested against the document, both model families agree on every field; DISAGREEMENT — reading uncertain, human review queued; MISMATCH — documented and booked terms differ; STALE — a prior attestation no longer applies because the document or booking changed since.
